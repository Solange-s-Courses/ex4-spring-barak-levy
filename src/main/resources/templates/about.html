About This Site

Welcome to our online electronic shopping site

This website is an online platform for selling electronic products. Users can register either as regular users who buy products or as company users who sell products to others.

The site supports full interaction between buyers and sellers, allowing registered companies to manage their product listings - adding a new product, edit the product or delete it.

The company can also see all the orders for their products that the users made and view the reviews from users. The regular users can browse the products, write a review, purchase products and see their orders history.

The platform also includes an admin who responsible for overseeing and moderating the system. Admins can manage users, products, categories, and reviews to ensure the platform remains secure and well organized.
